About Kay

A facilitator who has walked the walk

Most of the leaders I work with are capable, committed and quietly tired. They know the models. What they rarely get is the space to notice what is happening inside them while they lead.

That is where my talks begin. I have spent over thirty years in people development and leadership, from contact centres to boardrooms, leading one of the UK's largest transformation programmes and building leadership and sales academies used across 22 countries.

I have lived and worked in eight countries, including Colombia, Thailand, Vietnam and California. Those cultures shaped how I see leadership: as something rooted in community, connection and the courage to slow down.

My sessions are light on slides and heavy on conversation. I blend neuroscience, adult learning theory and coaching with real workplace scenarios, so people recognise themselves in the room and leave with a practice, not just a point of view.

Talks

What I speak about

Identity and the inner fraud

The New Leadership Imposter

Why so many capable leaders feel like they are about to be found out, and why it is rising as roles change faster than identities can keep up. Built on The Calibration Method and its four imposter archetypes.

Leaders name their own pattern and how it shows up

Tackling The Inner Fraud

A practical session on the voice that says you are not ready yet. Introduces Focal 3, Kay's technique for quietening it, and turns it into something people can use before their next big moment.

A technique to use the same week

Self regulation and resilience

Self Regulation For Teams

Building high performance from the inside out. The Window of Tolerance, why teams take their cue from the leader's state, and the four self regulation archetypes: Navigator, Anchor, Architect and Connector.

Every participant discovers their archetype

Self Regulation In A Modern World

First delivered as a masterclass for Manchester Metropolitan University. Covers always on working, energy and chronotype, the scripts we repeat, and a somatic reset that takes sixty seconds.

A daily reset people actually keep

Leadership Resilience

Resilience as something leaders build and model, not something they are expected to absorb. How pace, pressure and isolation wear leaders down, and the habits that keep them steady enough to think clearly and lead well.

Practical habits for steadier leadership under pressure

Modern leadership and learning

Learning As A Collective Ecosystem

From Kay's book. Why learning is not the sole responsibility of L&D, how the forest underground network explains where real learning moves, and what changes when leaders sit at the centre.

For operations directors, HR and L&D leaders

What Colombia Taught Me About True Leadership

Lessons from living alongside the Costeños and indigenous communities of northern Colombia: community, slowing down, gratitude, and a chief who works alongside their people.

A story led keynote with practical takeaways
